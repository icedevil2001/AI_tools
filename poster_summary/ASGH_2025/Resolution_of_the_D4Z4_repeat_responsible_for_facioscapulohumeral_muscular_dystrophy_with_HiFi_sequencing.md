**Introduction**  
The D4Z4 repeat is a variable number tandem repeat (VNTR) that contains some of the most difficult-to-resolve regions in the human genome. D4Z4 has a repeat unit of 3.3 kb encoding the DUX4 protein, implicated in facioscapulohumeral muscular dystrophy (FSHD). This research aims to understand D4Z4 through a computational approach and aims to produce a thorough resolution of D4Z4 using PacBio HiFi whole-genome sequencing.

**Application of KiTwi in FSHD Patient Samples**  
1. 11 FSHD1 samples, allele size known for normal/FSHD patients.  
2. 5 samples with SHG/WT variants.  
3. 6 samples with normal/SHG/WT.  
4. 473 population samples across five ancestral populations.  

**Kiwvi Workflow**
